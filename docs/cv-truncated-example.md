## Summary

Sedimentologist with 20+ years of experience in clastic and mixed-lithology deep-water systems. I integrate **outcrop analogs**, **digital outcrop models**, core, subsurface datasets, and modern-seafloor observations to characterize reservoir architecture and predict fluid flow. Director of the Geology Center of Research Excellence (CoRE) at Mines; 60 peer-reviewed publications; h-index 26.
